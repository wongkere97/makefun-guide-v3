# Notes on this guide

## How the referral flow works

Every make.fun account has a referral code. Mine is `j1fs5kh5`, and the link
https://make.fun/r/j1fs5kh5 points to it. When a new person opens that link and
creates an account, the signup is credited to my code. The bounty scores real,
verified signups, so a click alone does nothing, and a person who signs up
through someone else's link does not count for this guide.

The reader's side is simple: open the link, make a free account, read
[skill.md](https://make.fun/skill.md), then take a bounty. The reward for me is
a verified signup; the reward for the reader is a working start on the platform
and a share of whatever they earn.

## Why the CTA sits at the top

The previous guide had zero verified signups. The most likely reason is that a
reader had to hunt for the link, or never understood what signing up would get
them. So this version leads with one clear action: a bold "create your free
account" line right under the title, with the raw link and the code spelled out
a second time so nobody has to copy text out of a badge.

The ad page does the same thing with a single large button. Everything else on
that page, the headline, the three cards, the footer, exists to make the button
feel worth clicking. No external scripts or styles, so the page renders the
same offline and the screenshot stays faithful to what a reader sees.
